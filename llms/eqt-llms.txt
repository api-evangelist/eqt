# EQT Corporation

> EQT Corporation is the largest producer of natural gas in the United States, headquartered in
> Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. It is a vertically integrated Appalachian Basin operator: upstream
> production plus, since the July 2024 acquisition of Equitrans Midstream, interstate gathering and
> transmission pipelines. EQT does not run a developer program and publishes no API.

## Regulated data surface

- EQT Midstream Informational Postings Web Site: https://infopost.eqt.com/ — the FERC-required (18 CFR
  284.13) posting site for the Equitrans interstate pipelines. Sections follow the NAESB WGQ vocabulary:
  Capacity, Capacity Release, Index Of Customers, Nominations and Scheduling, Notices, Tariff. CSV and
  PDF export exist inside the application. It is a Salesforce Experience Cloud single-page app, so the
  postings are not reachable by a crawler or an agent without executing JavaScript, and the deeper
  posting routes return 401 to anonymous callers.
- Equitrans Midstream customer portal: https://customers.equitransmidstream.com/ — same platform,
  gathering and transmission customer services.

## Disambiguation

EQT Corporation (NYSE: EQT, natural gas, Pittsburgh PA) is a different company from EQT AB / EQT Group
(the Swedish private-equity firm at eqtgroup.com) and from EQS Group. Material about AI funds, ISO 27001
programs or DORA compliance under the name "EQT" generally belongs to EQT AB and must not be attributed
to EQT Corporation.
